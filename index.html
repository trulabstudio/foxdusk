        Are there really no monthly fees?

        There's no monthly fee to Foxdusk. Third-party services such as your domain and hosting have their own renewals, which you pay directly to the provider.

        What does the free support cover?

        For 6 months, it covers bug fixes and help using your system. New features and major changes are quoted separately.

        What happens after 6 months?

        Your system is yours and keeps running. If you need support or changes, we'll work to what you need and your budget.

        Do I own the system?

        Yes. The system and its code belong to you.

        Do I need to know exactly what I want?

        No. Tell us what's going wrong and we'll work out the right solution together.
