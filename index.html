        Are there really no monthly fees?

        There's no monthly fee to Foxdusk. Services you choose to connect, such as Claude or ElevenLabs, bill you separately under their own plans.

        How many computers can I use it on?

        One licence key works on 2 computers. Deactivate a computer in Foxdusk Studio to move it, or email us and we'll reset it.

        Do I need Claude Code?

        You need Claude Code or OpenAI Codex for motion graphics, reels and showreels. Transcription, cuts, captions and downloading your video work in Foxdusk Studio without either one.

        Can I use another AI instead of Claude Code?

        Yes, OpenAI Codex. Every Foxdusk skill comes in a Claude Code version and a Codex version, so you can use whichever plan you already pay for. Other AI tools, such as ChatGPT in the browser, Gemini or Cursor, aren't supported.

        What do I need to run it?

        A Mac or a Windows PC with at least 5 GB free, and an internet connection. Foxdusk downloads its own tools the first time.

        Do I need the internet?

        Yes. Foxdusk needs the internet to check your licence, and Claude Code or Codex work online. Your videos stay on your computer: Foxdusk only uploads them if you choose a service such as ElevenLabs.

        Can I use it for client work?

        Yes. Videos you make are yours, including for clients. You can't share, resell or pass on the kit itself.

        Can I get a refund?

        Payments are not refundable once your licence key has been sent. If you can't get it running, email us and we'll help.
