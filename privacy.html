Privacy policy

Last updated 1 November 2026

This policy explains how Foxdusk, a division of Trulab Production Sdn. Bhd. (Company No. 202301031762 (1525685-T)), handles your information when you visit this website or contact us.

What we receive

This website has no account or sign-up form, and it uses no analytics, advertising pixels or cookies. We only receive what you choose to send us by WhatsApp or email, such as your name, phone number, email address and message.

The Workflow X-ray runs in your browser. What you enter stays on your device unless you choose to send it to us.

How we use it

  • To reply to you and prepare a quote
  • To deliver and support work you ask us to do
  • To meet our legal and accounting duties

We don't sell your information. We keep it only as long as we need it for these purposes.

Services involved

Vercel hosts this website, and Google Fonts and jsDelivr deliver its fonts and code. These services may process technical details such as your IP address to deliver files to you. If you contact us, WhatsApp or your email provider also handles your message under its own terms.

Your browser may store public files from this website so that it can load when you're offline.

Your choices

You can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to stop using it, by emailing us. We handle these requests in line with Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010.
