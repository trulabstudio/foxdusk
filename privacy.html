Privacy policy

Last updated 5 November 2026

This policy explains how Foxdusk, a division of Trulab Production Sdn. Bhd. (Company No. 202301031762 (1525685-T)), handles your information when you visit this website, contact us, or buy Foxdusk AIOS.

What we receive

This website uses no analytics, advertising pixels or tracking cookies. We receive what you choose to send us by WhatsApp or email, such as your name, phone number, email address and message.

When you order Foxdusk AIOS, we receive your name, email address, phone number and the payment receipt you upload. When you activate Foxdusk AIOS, Foxdusk Studio sends us your licence key, a random ID for that computer, the computer's name and the app version, so we can limit each key to its allowed computers.

The Editing X-ray runs in your browser. What you enter stays on your device unless you choose to send it to us.

How we use it

  • To reply to your questions
  • To confirm your payment, send your licence key and download link, and support you
  • To check licence keys and stop keys that are shared or resold
  • To meet our legal and accounting duties

We don't sell your information. We keep it only as long as we need it for these purposes.

Services involved

Vercel hosts this website, and Google Fonts and jsDelivr deliver its fonts and code. These services may process technical details such as your IP address to deliver files to you. If you contact us, WhatsApp or your email provider also handles your message under its own terms.

For Foxdusk AIOS orders, Supabase stores your order details, receipt, licence and the download, and Resend delivers our emails to you. The admin sign-in page stores a sign-in session in the admin's browser only.

If you pay by card or FPX, Stripe processes the payment on its own secure page under its own terms. We never see or store your card details; Stripe tells us only that the payment succeeded.

The order form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to tell people from bots. It runs only on the order form and may process technical details such as your IP address and browser, under Cloudflare's own terms.

Your browser may store public files from this website so that it can load when you're offline.

Your choices

You can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to stop using it, by emailing us. We handle these requests in line with Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010.
